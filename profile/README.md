## What we do

Maven Solutions partners with companies to turn the promise of AI into measurable outcomes. We specialize in two tightly connected practices:

- **Agentic AI** — autonomous, tool-using agents that plan, reason, and execute multi-step workflows across your stack. From research copilots and customer-support agents to operations and sales automation, we ship agents that do real work, not demos.
- **AI Integrations** — production-grade connections between LLMs and the tools your teams depend on: CRMs, ERPs, data warehouses, ticketing systems, internal APIs, and custom business logic. Secure, observable, and built to scale.

## How we help

- **Strategy & discovery** — identify the highest-ROI AI opportunities in your business and map them to a pragmatic delivery plan.
- **Custom agent development** — build multi-agent systems with robust planning, memory, tool use, and guardrails.
- **LLM integration & orchestration** — connect OpenAI, Anthropic, and open-source models to your data and workflows via MCP, RAG, and custom pipelines.
- **Automation & workflow engineering** — replace brittle scripts and manual handoffs with intelligent, self-healing workflows.
- **Deployment & observability** — evaluations, monitoring, cost controls, and guardrails so your AI runs reliably in production.
